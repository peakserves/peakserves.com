How to delete your data

Brightstage, operated by PEAKSERVE GLOBAL PRIVATE LIMITED

If you connected an Instagram account

  1. In Instagram, open Settings → Apps and websites (or Website permissions → Apps and websites), find Brightstage, and remove it. This revokes our access immediately.
  2. Then email info@peakserves.com from any address with the subject “Delete my data – Brightstage” and your Instagram username.

If you are a creator we work with

Email info@peakserves.com or message us on WhatsApp with “Delete my data”. We will confirm your identity using the phone number or email we have on file.

If you commented on or messaged a connected account

Email info@peakserves.com with your Instagram username.

What happens next

Within 30 days we delete your tokens, profile data, comments and messages, uploads and metrics, and confirm by reply. We keep only what the law requires us to keep (for example payment records), and only for as long as required.
