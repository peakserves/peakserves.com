Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 10 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Brightstage collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how you can have it deleted. Brightstage is operated by PEAKSERVE GLOBAL PRIVATE LIMITED (trading as “PeakServes”), CIN U70200DL2023PTC414058, with its registered office at 2639/197, Onkar Nagar, Tri Nagar, Delhi, North West Delhi, India - 110035 (“we”, “us”). We act as the data fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDPA”). The privacy policy for the PeakServes website itself is here.

1. Who this applies to

  • Connected account holders: people who connect an Instagram professional account to Brightstage (our own team and creators we work with).
  • Creators: people we invite to make content for us, who receive briefs, upload videos and are messaged on WhatsApp.
  • People who interact with connected accounts: for example, people who comment on or send direct messages to a connected Instagram account.

3. AI assistance

We use AI tools to draft captions and suggested replies to comments and messages. The text of a comment or message may be sent to our AI provider to produce a draft. Drafts are reviewed according to each account's settings. Accounts that post AI-generated personas are disclosed as such on their profile.

4. Who we share data with

Only with service providers that run Brightstage for us, under their own terms and data protection commitments:

  • Meta Platforms (Instagram API, WhatsApp Business Platform), to publish, read and send on connected accounts.
  • Cloudflare (storage of media files).
  • Hetzner Online (application servers and database, Singapore region).
  • Anthropic (AI drafting, as described above).

Some of these providers process data outside India. We may disclose data if required by law.

5. How long we keep it

  • Access tokens: until you disconnect the account or revoke access.
  • Media prepared for publishing: deleted from public storage within 30 days.
  • Comments, messages, metrics and assignment records: for as long as the account or creator relationship is active, then deleted within 90 days, unless we must keep them longer for legal, tax or payment reasons.
  • Technical logs: up to 30 days.

6. Security

Tokens are encrypted at rest, creator uploads are kept in private storage, access to the admin tools is restricted to authorised team members, and all traffic uses HTTPS.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or nominate someone to exercise these rights for you, as provided by the DPDPA and other applicable law. See how to delete your data, or write to info@peakserves.com. We reply within 30 days.

8. Children

Brightstage is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page with a new effective date.
